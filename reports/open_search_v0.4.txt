Meta-pattern layer
------------------
Pattern reports are converted into first-class PatternObject records with abstract signatures, role graphs, assumptions, identities, concrete realizations, domains, and failure modes. The run found six bounded families:

- composition_chain — 12 independent realizations;
- continuous_or_discrete_analogue — 5 independent realizations;
- differential_complex_candidate — 3 independent realizations;
- missing_link_candidate — 5 independent realizations;
- two-step complex law (`next ∘ previous = 0`) — 4 independent realizations;
- operator_family — 6 independent realizations.

The family score combines independent-domain count, concrete realizations, assumption generality, and structural compression. Generic graph gaps are not promoted to predictions; predictions are emitted only from reusable complex or continuous/discrete meta-laws. The campaign produced 32 justified predicted-role records with confidence reasons and expected identities/assumptions.

Residual layer
--------------
Oracle failures now create first-class ResidualObject records containing domain, codomain, object kind, order, locality, dimension/metric/boundary/regularity/discretization dependence, convergence status, canonical form, and cluster key. Canonicalization ignores formatting and groups residuals by typed regime signature. The run formed 15 residual clusters and derived correction requirements such as boundary conditions, regularity-aware terms, and compatible discrete realizations.

Goal-directed synthesis
-----------------------
The grammar is activated by an explicit goal rather than by unrestricted enumeration. Supported justified rules include adjoint, projection/inclusion, weighted linear combination, and correction term. Across four cycles, 59 unique correction attempts were retained after canonical deduplication; the ordinary composition count stayed at 169. No ordinary valid composition became a serious candidate.
