Semantic gate
-----------
The campaign now applies a registry-backed semantic gate before a composition is treated as an open candidate. It recognizes Atlas identity rewrites and curated constructions including gradient-divergence/Laplacian, Hessian/Jacobian-gradient, curl-curl decomposition, Hodge-Laplacian, symmetric/skew decomposition, and Fourier correspondence. Eight generated candidates were therefore classified as known-equivalent rather than mathematical novelty.

Interestingness model
---------------------
Candidate dossiers expose semantic novelty, generalization power, recovery power, independent relations, compression reduction, invariant potential, cross-domain reach, computational utility, non-triviality, and evidence strength. The score is explanatory and prioritizes structural compression and reusable abstractions; it is not a probability of novelty.

Oracle layer
-----------
The regularity, boundary, geometry, dimension, and discretization oracles are assumption-sensitive. They record whether a candidate survives the supported regime, violates a declared regime, or is unsupported by the current backend. This run produced 21 failure patterns across 15 residual clusters, mostly dimension/boundary/regularity limitations of the current R3 symbolic/numerical slice. No executable mathematical counterexample property was supplied, so counterexamples found is zero.
