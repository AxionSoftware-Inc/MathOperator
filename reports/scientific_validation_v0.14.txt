1. Scope and architectural result

The v0.14 layer is implemented as a separate analogy module rather than being
folded into the Atlas or the existing discovery engines:

- include/opforge/analogy/engine.hpp
- src/analogy/engine.cpp
- src/cli/main.cpp

The module introduces first-class StructuralAnalogy, RoleMapping,
TransferredConstraint, TransferResidual, AnalogicalPrediction,
AnalogyBenchmarkCase, agenda, and A/B/C/D ablation objects. The Atlas remains
the source of typed mathematical facts; analogy reasoning consumes a frozen
Atlas snapshot and cannot silently add facts to it.

Mappings are typed and topology-aware. Names and symbols are not used as the
matching signal. Mapping evidence includes input/output object kind,
differential order, linearity, locality, continuous/discrete regime, required
structures, and relation degree. Competing target roles are retained for

The absence of rejected records in the bounded top-48 list is not treated as a
success claim: source-specific assumptions are rejected during transfer, and
negative controls are reported separately.

4. Constraint transfer and negative transfer

The engine transfers only typed role constraints, relation topology, and
composition arity by default. Source-only assumptions are marked
source_specific rather than copied into the target.

Negative-transfer benchmark:

- case: negative-transfer-metric-mismatch
- source: source_geometry
- target: target_geometry
- mismatch: source requires metric; target has no metric
- result: rejected
- residual: target metric or a correction is required
- negative transfers blocked: 1

This is a falsification result: a high-level role match is not sufficient to
authorize a law transfer.

8. Scientific interpretation

The v0.14 implementation establishes a reproducible internal structural-
analogy and constraint-transfer layer over the frozen v0.13 Atlas. It finds
typed analogies, transfers only justified constraints, hides direct bridges in
benchmarks, records negative transfer, and preserves residual evidence.

Scientific answer: one prospective cross-domain analogical prediction
succeeded internally; external checking remains gated. No novelty claim is
justified by this run.
