Derivation discipline
  Each prediction stores premises, structure, axiom, role substitution,
  minimal assumptions and a symbolic proof trace.
  Circular paths and visible-target reuse are rejected by the DAG/leakage gate.
  Derived facts are classified separately from explicitly stored and restated
  facts; no formal proof claim is made.
