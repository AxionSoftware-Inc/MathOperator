Diagnosis
---------
A. The engine mostly recovers structure already implicit in the Atlas; factorization leads confirm this.

B. It does infer higher-level repeated laws, especially the two-step-complex schema, but that abstraction is still structurally supported rather than mathematically novel.

C. It can repair the controlled projection failure without receiving the correction expression directly; open-ended leads do not yet generalize beyond their tested regime.

D. It predicts missing roles from meta-patterns, but hard-mode recovery misses show that the ontology and bridge evidence are not yet sufficient for robust hidden-role prediction.

E. Performance first fails at the numerical/counterexample backend and sparse ontology for generalized geometry, boundary, dimension, and regularity regimes. The synthesis grammar and ranking are secondary limitations.

Scientific conclusion
---------------------
v0.6 validates the evaluation discipline more strongly than it validates new mathematics: easy/medium/hard behavior is visible, leakage is zero, weak leads are not promoted, and hard failures remain reported. No result is called a new operator, theorem, or new mathematics.
